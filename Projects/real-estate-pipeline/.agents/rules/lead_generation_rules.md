# Lead Generation & ICP Rules

## Mandatory ICP Criteria
Every lead scraped or delivered in this workspace must fulfill all of the following criteria:
- **Company Size**: 10 – 30 employees
- **Order / Deal Value**: $7,000+ per order/deal
- **Lead Response / Timeline**: < 48 hours (Job posted < 48 hours ago)
- **Technical Knowledge**: Less experienced / Non-technical
- **Target Geography**: United States (US) & United Kingdom (UK)
- **Core Client Intent**: Actively seeking a Lead Generation Freelancer / Contractor / Partner

## Google Sheets Master Sheet Rule
- **Spreadsheet**: `https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1k8aPzpsR3E-yIGsjEpr87cGrzwhgHqUjw-SSagyxSiQ/edit`
- **Spreadsheet Title**: `Lead Generation ICP`
- **Master Tab**: `Master Leads`
- **Appending**: Always add new leads below old leads without deleting or replacing old leads.

## Link Validation Rule
- Every job link must be a direct, fully functional, clickable hyperlink leading straight to the live job posting.
