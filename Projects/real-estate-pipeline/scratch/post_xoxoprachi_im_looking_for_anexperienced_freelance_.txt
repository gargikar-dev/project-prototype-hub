I'm looking for an experienced freelance outbound specialist to fully own client acquisition for a content writing business. - This is not a VA role. - This is not a template-sending role. - This is for someone who already knows how outbound works and wants autonomy + upside. What you will own (end-to-end): ➡️ Choosing the outbound channel: - Cold email - LinkedIn - Or a hybrid approach ➡️ Building and validating lead lists - Writing and iterating outreach copy - Running follow-ups - Booking qualified sales calls You will not be micromanaged. ➡️ What success looks like: - Consistent conversions - Leads that match ICP (SaaS / B2B, B2C decision-makers) - Conversations that convert into retainers ➡️ Who You Are: - 2–5+ years experience in B2B outreach/sales development - Proficient in LinkedIn Sales Navigator & email outreach tools - Strong copywriting skills (cold email + LinkedIn messaging) - Data-driven and results-oriented If you meet the criteria, please fill in the form in the comments. I'll reach out if you're a fit. Strictly NO DMs.
