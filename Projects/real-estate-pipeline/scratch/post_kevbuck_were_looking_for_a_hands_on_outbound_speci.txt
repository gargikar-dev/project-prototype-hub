We’re looking for a hands-on outbound specialist. Someone who has actually booked meetings for a services business using LinkedIn + email. Not just set up tools. Not just strategy. Execution. You know how to: - Reach founders and get replies - Write messages that don’t sound like templates - Turn cold outreach into real conversations - Build pipeline without hiding behind automation This is a doer role, not a manager or “growth strategist.” If you’ve done this before and can show it, let’s talk. Dm Me!!
