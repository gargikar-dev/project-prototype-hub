I’m looking for a **Co-Founder / Growth Partner for MarketingKO.** Not an employee. Not a freelancer. Not someone looking for a fixed salary. I’m looking for someone who can **own lead generation and business development** and wants to build the company with me. [MarketingKO](https://in.linkedin.com/company/marketingko?trk=public_post-text) helps D2C brands automate their customer journey, improve speed-to-lead, qualify inquiries and recover lost conversions. The role You would own: • B2B lead generation • Prospect research and ICP building • Cold email / LinkedIn / outbound • Sales pipeline development • Booking qualified conversations • Testing new acquisition channels • Building repeatable lead-gen systems • Helping shape our go-to-market strategy Compensation This is **equity + performance based**. There is no fixed salary being promised at the beginning. If you generate qualified opportunities, close business and help grow the company, **you participate in the upside.** The exact equity and performance structure will be discussed based on commitment, experience and contribution. Who I’m looking for Someone who: • Has actually generated B2B leads before • Understands outbound, not just theory • Can pick up the phone and talk to prospects • Can handle rejection • Can work independently • Thinks like an owner • Is comfortable with uncertainty • Wants to build something long term And most importantly: **You need to be willing to say NO.** No to bad prospects. No to bad-fit clients. No to shortcuts. No to chasing vanity metrics. No to opportunities that don't make sense for the business. I would rather work with someone who says **“this isn't worth pursuing”** than someone who says yes to everything. This is not for everyone. If you're looking for a guaranteed salary and a defined 9–5 role, **please don't apply.** If you want to build, experiment, take ownership and share the upside, let's talk. Comment **“COFOUNDER”** or DM me with: What you've built/sold before Your experience with lead generation How you would get the first 100 qualified D2C prospects for MarketingKO Why you want to build rather than just work I’m intentionally going to be selective. **I’m willing to say no.**
