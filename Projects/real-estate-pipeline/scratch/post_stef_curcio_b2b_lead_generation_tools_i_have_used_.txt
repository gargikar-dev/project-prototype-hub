B2B Lead Generation tools I have used and would recommend: 1) Sales Navigator It is hands down the most comprehensive B2B database out there. It is the single subscription on this list that is a MUST have. 2) Lemlist Cold emailing CRM software that allows you to generate multi-channel campaigns. It integrates with Hubspot, and Salesforce among other CRM. It has one of the best customer services I have ever experienced. 2) Dropcontact Good data scraping software. Not sure it is the best option out there but it integrates well with Lemlist. 3) Phantombuster It allows you to automate some tasks like putting all the personal URLs of a sales navigator search into a CSV file that you can then use to scrape emails. 4) Octopus CRM* To be honest, when it comes to LinkedIn automation software I have tried many, and most of them were good. Octopus CRM is the last one I used. I would not recommend using it unless you learn how to automate your outreach without hurting your brand first. *Currently not using it. Is there any other software that is a must-have for any B2B lead gen outreach strategy?
