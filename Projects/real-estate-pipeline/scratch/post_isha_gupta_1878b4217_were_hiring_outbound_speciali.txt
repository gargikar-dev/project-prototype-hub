🚀 We’re Hiring: Outbound Specialist / Lead Generation Executive 🚀 We’re looking for a hungry, proactive Outbound Specialist who loves starting conversations, opening doors, and generating quality leads. If you enjoy outreach, follow-ups, and turning “cold” into “interested” - this one’s for you. What you’ll do: Identify and research potential clients Run outbound campaigns (LinkedIn, email, calls, WhatsApp) Qualify leads and book meetings for the sales team Maintain and update lead data in CRM Continuously improve outreach scripts and strategies What we’re looking for: 1-3 years experience in outbound sales or lead generation Strong written and verbal communication Comfortable with LinkedIn outreach, email tools, and CRMs Goal-oriented, consistent, and self-driven B2B / tech services experience Why join us? Fast-growing tech & services company Direct impact on revenue and growth Opportunity to grow into senior sales roles Supportive, performance-driven culture 📩 Interested?
