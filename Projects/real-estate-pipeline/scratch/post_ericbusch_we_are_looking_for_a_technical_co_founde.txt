We are looking for a technical co-founder. We are launching [leadfacto.ai](http://leadfacto.ai/?trk=public_post-text) — an AI-powered lead generation platform for recruitment & staffing agencies. The product is built. The market is massive (185K+ agencies in the US & UK, no affordable alternative). We launch in September. What's missing: the person who owns the data engine and the architecture. Not a developer — a founding engineer with equity, full technical ownership, and a seat at the table. Senior backend/data profile, freelance-friendly, remote (Europe). Part-time to start is fine. Details in the job post below. Or DM me directly. One placement pays for a year of our tool. Imagine the market.
