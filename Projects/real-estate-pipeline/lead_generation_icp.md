# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) - Real Estate Lead Generation

This document defines the criteria and profile for qualifying ideal clients / leads in this project. All future lead qualification, outreach workflows, and targeting strategies will refer to the parameters specified below.

---

## 🎯 ICP Criteria Summary

| Attribute | Criteria / Target Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Company Size (Employees)** | **10 – 30 employees** |
| **Order / Deal Value** | **$7,000+** (Per order / deal value) |
| **Lead Response / Timeline** | **< 48 hours** (Fast turnaround & decision timeline) |
| **Technical Knowledge** | **Less experienced / Non-technical** (Requires streamlined, done-for-you, or intuitive solutions) |
| **Target Geography / Location** | **United States (US) & United Kingdom (UK)** |
| **Core Client Intent / Need** | **Actively seeking a Lead Generation Freelancer / Contractor / Partner**, or needs someone to generate leads & book appointments for them. |

---

## 🔍 Detailed Profile Breakdown

### 1. Company Size & Headcount
- **Range:** 10 to 30 employees.
- **Context:** Small to mid-sized teams (e.g., boutique real estate agencies, brokerage teams, or property management firms) that have active operational capacity but lack large in-house technical/marketing departments.

### 2. Transaction / Order Value
- **Threshold:** $7k+ per order/deal.
- **Context:** High-ticket transactions ensuring strong customer lifetime value (LTV) and high ROI on acquired leads.

### 3. Lead Velocity & Timeline
- **Window:** Less than 48 hours.
- **Context:** High-intent leads requiring immediate engagement, fast follow-up, and rapid conversion cycles.

### 4. Technical Maturity
- **Level:** Less experienced / Low technical proficiency.
- **Context:** Communication, onboarding, and lead delivery must be straightforward, low-friction, and not require complex technical setups on their end.

### 5. Geographical Focus
- **Regions:** United States (US) and United Kingdom (UK).
- **Context:** Primary target markets for real estate lead generation campaigns and client acquisition.

### 6. Client Intent & Need for Lead Generation
- **Target Persona:** Founders, Managing Directors, Head of Acquisitions, and Boutique Agency Owners who are looking for a **lead generation freelancer**, appointment setter, or independent contractor to build an outbound client pipeline.
- **Strict Project Rule:** Whenever scraping or prospect gathering is triggered in this project, leads MUST adhere to these ICP parameters (US/UK, 10–30 team size, high-ticket deal value, needing someone to generate leads).
