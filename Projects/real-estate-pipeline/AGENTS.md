# Project Lead Generation Rules & Operating Instructions

## 🎯 Mandatory ICP Criteria Summary

All leads gathered or scraped in this project **MUST strictly satisfy ALL** of the following ICP criteria before being presented, exported, or pasted:

| Attribute | Criteria / Target Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Company Size (Employees)** | **10 – 30 employees** |
| **Order / Deal Value** | **$7,000+** (Per order / deal value) |
| **Lead Response / Timeline** | **< 48 hours** (Fast turnaround & decision timeline / Job posted < 48 hours ago) |
| **Technical Knowledge** | **Less experienced / Non-technical** (Requires streamlined, done-for-you, or intuitive solutions) |
| **Target Geography / Location** | **United States (US) & United Kingdom (UK)** |
| **Core Client Intent / Need** | **Actively seeking a Lead Generation Freelancer / Contractor / Partner**, or needs someone to generate leads & book appointments for them. |

---

## 📋 Lead Qualification & Pre-Check Mandate
Before delivering, exporting, or pasting any leads into Google Sheets or local files:
1. **Verification**: Cross-check every single lead against the 6 criteria above. If any lead does not meet all criteria, discard it and replace it with a qualified one.
2. **Posting Velocity**: Ensure postings are fresh and strictly under 48 hours old.

## 📊 Single Master Google Sheet Protocol
This entire project uses **ONE single Google Sheet only**:

- **Master Sheet Tab**: `Master Leads`

### Appending Rule (Strict: No Overwrite):
- Whenever asked to generate leads again, **always open this exact Google Sheet**.
- **Append & Arrange Below Existing Leads**: Always add and arrange the new leads directly below the last row of existing leads.
- **Never delete, wipe, or replace old leads**. Preserve all previous records intact.

---

## 🔗 Clickable Direct Job Links Mandate
- When providing links in Google Sheets, Excel (`.xlsx`), CSV, or Markdown:
  - Every link **MUST be a valid, direct, and clickable hyperlink**.
  - Clicking the link **MUST navigate directly to the specific live job posting** (e.g., `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/...` or `https://uk.linkedin.com/jobs/view/...`).
  - Never use broken, truncated, generic search URLs, or unlinked plain text.
