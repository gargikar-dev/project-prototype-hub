# Competitive Audit Template & White Space Recipes

Ready-to-use markdown schemas for logging competitor intelligence and mapping white space.

---

## 1. Competitor Analysis Markdown Template (`competitor-analysis.md`)

```markdown
# Competitor Landscape & White Space Audit: [Brand Name]
*Generated on: YYYY-MM-DD | Focus Platform: Instagram / Multi-Channel*

---

## 1. Competitor Roster & Overview

| Competitor Handle | Category | Est. Followers | Est. Cadence | Median Public ER (%) | Primary Content Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **@RivalOne** | Direct | 45k | 4 posts/wk | 0.82% | 60-sec Reels |
| **@CompetitorB** | Direct | 120k | Daily | 0.35% | Static Quotes & Single Images |
| **@LeaderAlpha** | Aspirational | 350k | 5 posts/wk | 1.45% | In-depth Educational Carousels |
| **@AdjacentBrand** | Adjacent | 80k | 3 posts/wk | 2.10% | Contrarian Visual Teardowns |

---

## 2. Public Signals & Content Breakdown

### Competitor 1: @RivalOne (Direct)
- **Positioning / Bio Wedge**: *"We help coaches scale to 6-figures."*
- **Primary Content Pillars**: Motivational quotes (40%), Client testimonials (30%), General tips (30%).
- **Top Outlier Post Analysis**: A carousel comparing two software tools got $5\times$ normal comments because it took a clear side.
- **Key Vulnerability**: Weak comments engagement (zero founder replies); bio links to a dead booking page.

---

## 3. The White Space Opportunity Matrix

| Dimension | Competitor Orthodox Standard | The Open White Space (Our Wedge) | How We Win |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Topic Angle** | Generic "scale your business" platitudes | Technical, step-by-step DM workflow blueprints | Publish exact ManyChat flow teardowns |
| **Visual Style** | Generic Canva templates & stock photos | Clean 8-bit retro pixel graphics / bespoke diagrams | Instantly recognizable visual identity |
| **POV / Stance** | Safe, agreeable, middle-of-the-road advice | Contrarian: "Why cold outreach is killing agency retention" | Polarize and attract high-conviction buyers |
| **Conversion CTA** | Vague "DM me for info" | Keyword-triggered automated lead magnet delivery | Instant comment-to-DM automated flows |

---

## 4. Strategic Counter-Move Recommendations
1. **Double Down on Carousels**: Direct rivals only post low-effort reels; our 10-slide high-density carousels will capture 80% of saved bookmarks.
2. **Own the 'Automation Mechanics' Topic**: No competitor is publishing actual logic diagrams; this establishes immediate category authority.
```
