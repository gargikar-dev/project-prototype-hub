# Scope, Ethical Guidelines & Inter-Agent Connections

Rules of engagement for competitive intelligence gathering and inter-agent routing.

---

## 1. Ethical Reconnaissance Guidelines

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Permitted Reconnaissance**:
> - ✅ Auditing publicly visible profiles, handles, feeds, reels, comments, and links.
> - ✅ Analyzing public ads via the Meta Ad Library and TikTok Creative Center.
> - ✅ Calculating engagement rates from public counts (likes, comments).

> [!CAUTION]
> **Strictly Prohibited**:
> - ❌ Never attempt to access private competitor accounts, direct messages, or backend analytics.
> - ❌ Never fabricate metrics or claim insider knowledge of competitor revenue or ad spend.
> - ❌ Never clone or plagiarize text copy, hooks, or image designs.

---

## 2. Distinguishing the 5 Social Lenses

| Skill / Agent | Core Focus | Key Question Answered |
|---|---|---|
| **`competitor-analysis` (This)** | The Landscape | *"Where is the market white space across all competitors?"* |
| **`viral-reverse-engineering`** | Individual Post Deconstruction | *"Why did this single specific post get 1M views?"* |
| **`audience-research`** | Your Target Customer | *"What are our audience's verbatim pains and JTBD?"* |
| **`trend-jacking`** | Timely Cultural Moments | *"Can we ethically draft on this current viral meme/topic?"* |
| **`social-strategy`** | Your Overarching Plan | *"Which channels and objectives should we commit to?"* |
