# Competitor Benchmarks & Social Intelligence Realities (2026)

Guidelines on calculating accurate engagement rates and understanding cross-platform benchmark disparities.

---

## 1. 2026 Industry Benchmark Baselines

Median engagement rates vary significantly by platform architecture:

| Platform | 2026 Median Engagement Rate (Follower-Based) | Calculation Basis / Denominator |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | $\sim 3.70\%$ | Total Engagements / Total Followers |
| **Instagram** | $\sim 0.48\%$ – $0.65\%$ | (Likes + Comments + Saves + Shares) / Followers |
| **Facebook** | $\sim 0.15\%$ | Total Interactions / Page Followers |
| **X (Twitter)** | $\sim 0.12\%$ | Engagements / Followers |
| **LinkedIn** | $\sim 3.5\% - 5.0\%$ *(Per Impression)* | **Caution**: Measured per **Impression**, NOT per follower |

> [!WARNING]
> **Never Mix Denominators**: Do not compare a LinkedIn impression-based engagement rate ($4\%$) to an Instagram follower-based rate ($0.5\%$). Always benchmark within the same platform formula.

## 2. Public Engagement Calculation Formulas

### Formula 1: Follower-Based Engagement Rate (Standard Public Audit)
$$\text{ER}_{\text{follower}} = \left( \frac{\text{Average Likes} + \text{Average Comments per Post}}{\text{Total Follower Count}} \right) \times 100$$

### Formula 2: Share of Voice (SOV) Estimate in Direct Set
$$\text{SOV}_{\text{engagement}} = \left( \frac{\text{Your Total Monthly Engagements}}{\sum \text{Direct Competitor Set Monthly Engagements}} \right) \times 100$$

## 3. Competitive SWOT Integration

Wrap up every competitive audit with a 4-quadrant SWOT focused strictly on social positioning:
- **Strengths**: What are competitors genuinely great at (production value, posting speed)?
- **Weaknesses**: What are they lazy with (ignored comments, generic copy, weak link CTAs)?
- **Opportunities**: Which high-intent keyword gaps or unmet JTBD themes are open?
- **Threats**: Are rivals running heavy paid ad spend or aggressive influencer takeovers?
