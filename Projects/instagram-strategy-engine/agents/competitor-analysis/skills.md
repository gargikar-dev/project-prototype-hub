# Agent Skills: Competitor Analysis

## 1. Assigned Skills & Capabilities
- **Competitor Analysis Foundation Skill (`competitor-analysis-skill.md`)**:
  - *Description*: Executes landscape audits, public signal capture, engagement rate benchmarking, white space mapping, and strategic translation.
- **The SCOUT Reconnaissance Methodology**:
  - *Description*: Structures competitive discovery across direct, aspirational, and adjacent competitor sets.
- **White Space & Gap Analysis**:
  - *Description*: Identifies underserved thematic, format, POV, and funnel gaps across competitors' feeds.
- **Public Ad & Content Reverse-Engineering**:
  - *Description*: Audits public Meta Ad Library assets, organic hooks, posting frequencies, and CTA structures.

## 2. Tools & MCP Integrations
- [x] Workspace File Access (Reading `brand-profile.md`, `audience.md`, and writing `competitor-analysis.md`)
- [x] Web Reader & Search (Reviewing public competitor feeds, profiles, and public ad libraries)
- [x] Trend & Competitor Benchmarking Tools (Calculating median engagement rates and share-of-voice estimates)

## 3. Skill Execution Workflow
1. **Step 0 (Read Foundation)**: Ingest `brand-profile.md` and `audience.md` to anchor research against real target demand.
2. **Step 1 (Select the Field)**: Choose 5–8 competitors spanning Direct, Aspirational, and Adjacent categories.
3. **Step 2 (Capture Public Signals)**: Audit competitor positioning, themes, formats, cadence, hooks, visuals, and public ads.
4. **Step 3 (Observe Performance & Engagement)**: Analyze median engagement rates, comment quality, and share-of-voice without vanity metrics.
5. **Step 4 (Uncover the White Space)**: Construct the White Space Matrix mapping unaddressed audience pains and missing formats.
6. **Step 5 (Translate to Strategy)**: Translate findings into distinct brand counter-moves and hand off to `social-strategy` and `content-pillars`.
