# Audience Profile Template (`audience.md`)

Use this standard markdown schema when generating `audience.md`. Keep formatting tight, scannable, and evidence-grounded.

```markdown
# Audience Profile: [Brand / Project Name]
*Generated on: YYYY-MM-DD | Foundation: [Link to brand-profile.md]*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Transformation
- **Primary Segment**: [One-line description of the main audience segment]
- **Core Transformation**:
  - **Before**: [Current painful, inefficient, or frustrated state]
  - **After**: [Empowered, streamlined, or victorious desired state]
- **Evidence Confidence**: [High (Direct Customer Data & Reviews) / Moderate (Community & Public Mining) / Hypothesis (To be validated)]

---

## 2. Priority Audience Segments (1–3 Max)

### Segment 1: [Segment Name] (Primary)
- **Role Differentiation**:
  - **Follower (Practitioner / Content Consumer)**: [Who follows, shares, and consumes social posts]
  - **Buyer (Decision Maker / Budget Holder)**: [Who signs off on purchase / approves contract, if different]
- **Sophistication Level**: [Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced / Cynical Expert]
- **Primary Channels & Mindset**:
  - **Instagram**: [e.g., Casual discovery, looking for actionable carousels & relatable reels during commute]
  - **LinkedIn / Other**: [e.g., Tactical career advice, industry case studies]

#### Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)
- **Functional Job**: [What specific task or process are they trying to complete?]
- **Emotional Job**: [How do they want to feel / stop feeling?]
- **Social Job**: [How do they want to be perceived by peers / managers / followers?]

#### Key Pains & Friction Points
1. [Pain 1 - Specific bottleneck or frustration]
2. [Pain 2 - Wasted resource / cost / anxiety]
3. [Pain 3 - Existing inadequate workaround]

#### Core Desires & Desired Outcomes
1. [Outcome 1 - Measurable or concrete result]
2. [Outcome 2 - Emotional payoff / freedom]

#### Pre-existing Beliefs & Content Triggers
- **What they currently believe**: [Common industry myth or assumption they hold]
- **Belief shift needed**: [The perspective our content must unlock]
- **Key Objections to pre-empt**: [Why they hesitate to adopt our solution or advice]

---

## 3. Voice of Customer (VoC) Language Bank

> *Rule: These are verbatim phrases mined from real customer sources, Reddit, reviews, or DMs.*

### A. How They Describe the Problem (Verbatim Quotes)
- *"[Quote 1: Problem in their exact words]"*
- *"[Quote 2: Emotional reaction / daily headache]"*
- *"[Quote 3: Breaking point phrase]"*

### B. How They Describe What They Want (Dream State Quotes)
- *"[Quote 1: Ideal outcome in everyday words]"*
- *"[Quote 2: The relief they are seeking]"*

### C. Objections & Cynicism (Verbatim Doubts)
- *"[Quote 1: Why they might distrust or delay]"*
- *"[Quote 2: Past bad experience with alternatives]"*

---

## 4. Content Application Guidelines
- **High-Converting Hook Angles**:
  - *Angle 1 (Pain callout)*: "[Sample hook formula based on JTBD]"
  - *Angle 2 (Belief challenge)*: "[Sample hook formula challenging common assumption]"
- **Topics & Themes to Prioritize**: [Specific subjects that trigger high engagement/shares]
- **Words/Jargon to Avoid**: [Corporate or unnatural buzzwords that kill engagement]
```
