# Voice of Customer (VoC) Mining & Language Bank Construction

The highest-converting copy doesn't invent clever phrases; it mirrors the audience's exact words back to them. When someone reads their own unexpressed thoughts on a screen, they instantly assume you have the solution.

---

## 1. Primary Sources for VoC Mining

| Channel | What to Look For | Search Query Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Reddit & Online Forums** | Unfiltered rants, "how do I solve...", workarounds, tool critiques | `subreddit:marketing "hate when" OR "wasting time"`<br>`subreddit:entrepreneur "biggest headache"` |
| **G2 / Capterra / Amazon Reviews** | 2-star and 3-star reviews (goldmine for real frustrations without pure toxicity) | Read the "Cons" and "What problems is this solving?" sections |
| **YouTube & TikTok Comments** | Timestamps where viewers get confused, objections to creator advice | *"I tried this but...", "What about when...", "This only works if..."* |
| **Support Tickets & DMs** | Verbatim customer friction, hesitation during onboarding | Questions asked repeatedly right before purchasing or churning |
| **Sales Call Transcripts** | The exact phrases prospects use when asked *"What triggered you to look for a solution today?"* | Gong / Chorus logs, CRM notes, discovery notes |

---

## 2. Extraction Technique: Verbatim vs. Paraphrase

> [!CAUTION]
> **Never normalize user quotes into clean marketing prose.**
> - ❌ *Paraphrased*: "Customers desire an automated solution to streamline their inbound communications."
> - ✅ *Verbatim VoC*: *"I feel like a hostage to my Instagram DMs every time a reel goes viral."*

### What to Capture:
1. **Problem Triggers**: How they describe the breaking point that forced action.
2. **Emotional Reactions**: Guilt, anxiety, embarrassment, relief, pride.
3. **Metaphors & Slang**: Specific shorthand terms they use with peers.
4. **Competitor Teardowns**: Why existing alternatives let them down.

---

## 3. The Language Bank Structure

Store extracted quotes into structured categories inside `audience.md`:

```markdown
### Language Bank

#### 1. Pain & Frustration Phrases (Verbatim)
- *"I'm spending 3 hours every evening just copying and pasting link replies."*
- *"Half my leads drop off because by the time I reply 8 hours later, they've moved on."*
- *"I hate feeling like I can't take a Sunday off without losing sales."*

#### 2. Desired Outcomes & "Dream State"
- *"I just want to wake up and see qualified calls booked while I was asleep."*
- *"A system that feels personal without requiring me to type on my phone all day."*

#### 3. Skepticism & Objections
- *"Does this make my profile look like an automated spam bot?"*
- *"Will Meta flag my account if I trigger 500 DMs in an hour?"*
- *"I don't have time to learn complex workflow builders with 50 nodes."*
```
