# Audience Research Profiles: Examples Across Business Models

This file demonstrates the difference between **Useless Demographic Personas** and **Actionable Jobs-to-be-Done Audience Profiles**.

---

## Example 1: B2B Creator / Solopreneur Automation Tool

### ❌ Useless Demographic Persona
> *"Alex, 29, Digital Creator living in Austin. Uses MacBook Pro, drinks oat milk lattes, wants to grow his Instagram audience to 100k."*

### ✅ Actionable JTBD & VoC Profile
- **Primary Segment**: Solo info-product creator selling $49–$299 courses and digital templates.
- **Roles**: Buyer and Follower are the same person (solo operator).
- **Sophistication Level**: Intermediate (already has 5k–30k followers, produces reels regularly, overwhelmed by manual DM fulfillment).
- **Jobs-to-be-Done**:
  - *Functional*: Send free PDF guide links automatically whenever followers comment "GUIDE" on a Reel.
  - *Emotional*: Eliminate Sunday evening guilt of having 200 unread DMs sitting unanswered.
  - *Social*: Appear professional and instantly responsive like top 1% creators.
- **Language Bank (VoC)**:
  - *"I'm glued to my phone every time a post hits Explore."*
  - *"I lost a $500 client because I took 14 hours to reply to an Instagram DM."*
  - *"I don't want a complex enterprise tool that takes 3 weeks to wire up."*
- **Content Hooks**:
  - *"How to turn every 'PDF' comment into an automated email lead without touching your phone."*
  - *"The 3 DM automation flows that made me $4,200 while I was on a flight."*

## Example 2: B2B SaaS (HR / People Ops Platform)

### ❌ Useless Demographic Persona
> *"Michael, 42, VP of People in Chicago. Drives an SUV, attends HR conferences, cares about employee happiness."*

### ✅ Actionable JTBD & VoC Profile
- **Segments**:
  - **The Follower (HR Manager / People Ops Lead)**: Executes onboarding, handles exit interviews, scrolls LinkedIn/Instagram for practical templates and sanity checks.
  - **The Buyer (VP of HR / CFO)**: Signs annual software contracts, needs compliance certainty and retention ROI.
- **Jobs-to-be-Done**:
  - *Functional (Follower)*: Cut new hire onboarding paperwork from 4 days to 45 minutes.
  - *Emotional (Follower)*: Avoid being blamed when a new engineer's laptop and credentials aren't ready on Day 1.
  - *Social (Buyer)*: Present executive-ready retention reports to the board without spending a weekend in Excel.
- **Language Bank (VoC)**:
  - *"Our current onboarding is a frankenstein monster of Google Docs and Slack messages."*
  - *"I feel like an admin robot rather than doing real people strategy."*
- **Content Hooks**:
  - *"The Day 1 onboarding checklist that stopped new engineers from quitting in their first 90 days."*
  - *"Why your onboarding is secretly costing you $18,000 per lost hire."*
