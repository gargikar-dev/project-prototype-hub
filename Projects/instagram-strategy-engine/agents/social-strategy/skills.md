# Agent Skills: Social Strategy

## 1. Assigned Skills & Capabilities
- **Social Strategy Foundation Skill (`social-strategy.md`)**:
  - *Description*: Formulates high-level social strategy, scoring channels, setting business objectives, engineering the strategic wedge, and structuring the operating cadence.
- **Channel Selection & Trade-Off Scoring**:
  - *Description*: Evaluates candidate platforms across 4 criteria: Audience Presence, Format Fit, Goal Fit, and Capacity Fit.
- **Strategic Wedge & Differentiation Engineering**:
  - *Description*: Synthesizes brand POV, distinctive formats, and contrarian perspectives into an unfair content advantage.
- **Operating Model & Content Engine Design**:
  - *Description*: Outlines sustainable production rhythms, cross-repurposing pipelines, and phased platform rollouts.

## 2. Tools & MCP Integrations
- [x] Workspace File Access (Reading `brand-profile.md`, `audience.md`, and writing `social-strategy.md`)
- [x] Web Reader & Trend Research (Analyzing competitor channel footprint and industry benchmarks)
- [x] Conversational Strategy Interviews (Interactive probing of business constraints and capacity)

## 3. Skill Execution Workflow
1. **Step 0 (Read Foundation)**: Load `brand-profile.md`, `audience.md`, and existing content assets.
2. **Step 1 (Set Objective)**: Select 1 primary business goal (awareness, leads, sales, authority) and meaningful native KPIs.
3. **Step 2 (Channel Fit Evaluation)**: Score candidate channels, designate 1 primary channel (+ at most 1–2 secondary), and name dropped platforms.
4. **Step 3 (Define Wedge)**: Articulate the unique stance, format innovation, or perspective that wins attention.
5. **Step 4 (Operating Model & Cadence)**: Establish realistic weekly posting frequency, content engine workflows, and phased expansion.
6. **Step 5 (Produce Artifact)**: Generate `social-strategy.md` following standard schema and hand off to `content-pillars`.
