# Business Goals & Measurement Framework

A social media presence with no business goal optimizes for algorithm dopamine instead of revenue and pipeline.

---

## 1. The Hierarchy of Social Media Objectives

Select **one primary objective** and at most **one secondary objective**:

| Objective | Business Context | Meaningful Metric (Signal) | Vanity Metric (Noise) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness & Reach** | Pre-launch, new brand, entering new category | Non-follower reach, content saves, outbound shares | Total impressions, follower count |
| **Authority & Positioning** | B2B service, high-ticket consulting, agency | Profile visit rate, high-intent DM conversations, bookmarks | Likes, superficial comment emojis |
| **Audience Growth** | Creator, media brand, community-led platform | Qualified follower growth velocity, newsletter conversion | Follow/unfollow spikes, giveaway followers |
| **Lead Generation** | B2B SaaS, course creators, service providers | Keyword comment $\rightarrow$ DM trigger rate, lead magnet opt-ins | Link clicks with 95% bounce rate |
| **Direct Sales** | E-commerce, D2C, physical retail | Add-to-cart clicks, promo code redemptions, revenue | Post likes |

---

## 2. Measurement Reality & Integrity

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Measurement Principle**:
> - Never invent non-existent third-party analytics dashboards.
> - Track performance through native in-app analytics (Instagram Professional Dashboard, LinkedIn Analytics, YouTube Studio).
> - Review weekly and monthly: Focus on high-signal qualitative feedback (who is commenting, what DMs are coming in) alongside quantitative conversion ratios.
