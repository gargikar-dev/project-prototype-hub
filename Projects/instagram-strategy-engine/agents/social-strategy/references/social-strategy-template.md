# Social Media Strategy Template (`social-strategy.md`)

Use this standard markdown schema when generating `social-strategy.md`.

```markdown
# Social Media Strategy: [Brand / Project Name]
*Generated on: YYYY-MM-DD | Foundation: brand-profile.md & audience.md*

---

## 1. Executive Strategy Summary
- **Primary Business Goal**: [Awareness / Authority / Audience Growth / Leads / Sales]
- **Target Metric (North Star)**: [Specific meaningful metric, e.g., Qualified Inbound DMs per week]
- **Core Strategic Wedge**: [One-line summary of the distinct angle, format, or POV that wins]

---

## 2. Channel Architecture & Fit Scoring

### A. Primary Channel (The Anchor — 70% Effort)
- **Platform**: [Instagram / LinkedIn / YouTube / etc.]
- **Primary Role**: [e.g., Conversion engine & visual proof showcase]
- **Why It Won (4-Fit Score)**:
  - *Audience Presence*: [Evidence of audience density]
  - *Format Fit*: [Match to carousel/video strength]
  - *Goal Fit*: [Direct alignment with DM lead automation]
  - *Capacity Fit*: [Team bandwidth to maintain 4 posts/week]

### B. Secondary Channel (Repurpose Only — 30% Effort)
- **Platform**: [e.g., LinkedIn]
- **Repurposing Workflow**: [e.g., Transform IG carousels into PDF slide decks]

### C. Deliberately Dropped Platforms
- **[Dropped Platform 1]**: [Rationale, e.g., Low audience intent, video production bottleneck]
- **[Dropped Platform 2]**: [Rationale, e.g., Misaligned demographic, channel saturation]

---

## 3. The Strategic Wedge & Differentiation
- **The Stance / POV**: [What strong belief does this brand stake out that competitors avoid?]
- **Signature Format**: [e.g., Deep teardown carousels with 8-bit visuals and actionable cheat sheets]
- **Tone & Delivery**: [How the voice translates into scroll-stopping hooks]

---

## 4. Operating Model & Cadence

### Weekly Production Rhythm
- **Primary Channel**: [X] posts/week ([Days, e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri]) + [Y] Stories/week
- **Secondary Channel**: [X] repurposed posts/week
- **Engagement Commitment**: [X] mins/day active commenting and DM qualification

### Phased Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Months 1–3)**: Focus exclusively on validating primary channel resonance and lead generation funnel.
- **Phase 2 (Months 4–6)**: Automate cross-repurposing to secondary channel; double down on top 20% viral formats.
```
