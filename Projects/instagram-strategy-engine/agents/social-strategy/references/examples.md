# Social Strategy Examples Across Business Archetypes

---

## Example 1: B2B Instagram Automation Agency

### Strategy Overview
- **Primary Objective**: Qualified Lead Generation (Inbound agency client discovery calls).
- **North Star Metric**: 15+ Qualified Inbound DMs per week from verified B2B agency owners.
- **Strategic Wedge**: Deconstruct exact DM automation workflows with live screens, showing unvarnished setup mechanics and real conversion numbers.

### Channel Selection
- **Primary Channel**: **Instagram** (High DM intent, ideal for carousel education + comment-to-DM automated flows).
- **Secondary Channel**: **LinkedIn** (Repurpose carousel slides as PDF document sliders).
- **Dropped Channels**:
  - *TikTok*: Dropped (Audience skews consumer; short-form talking head video takes 3x the production time without driving high-ticket enterprise contracts).
  - *YouTube*: Dropped for Phase 1 (High barrier to entry; deferred until Phase 3).

### Operating Model
- **Cadence**: 4 carousels/week on Instagram (Mon, Tue, Thu, Sat); 4 stories/week; 2 repurposed PDF sliders/week on LinkedIn.
- **Phasing**: Phase 1 (Months 1–3) on Instagram only $\rightarrow$ Phase 2 (Months 4–6) LinkedIn expansion.

## Example 2: D2C E-Commerce Brand (Sustainable Coffee Roaster)

### Strategy Overview
- **Primary Objective**: Direct Sales & Subscription Conversions.
- **North Star Metric**: Monthly promo code orders driven by Instagram Shop & Link-in-Bio.
- **Strategic Wedge**: Behind-the-scenes farm direct-trade transparency combined with 30-second aesthetic brewing recipes.

### Channel Selection
- **Primary Channel**: **Instagram** (Visual Reels + Stories for daily brewing culture + direct shop link).
- **Secondary Channel**: **TikTok** (Short-form video repurposing).
- **Dropped Channels**:
  - *LinkedIn*: Dropped (Irrelevant for direct retail coffee drinkers).
  - *X (Twitter)*: Dropped (Low visual format conversion).
