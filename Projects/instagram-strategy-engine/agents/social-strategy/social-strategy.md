# Social Strategy: Squarespace

*Last Updated: 2026-08-30 | Status: Active Strategy*

---

## 1. Primary Strategic Objective
- **Core Objective**: **Qualified Consideration & Product Adoption** (Driving profile visitors to launch websites, start free trials, and claim free `bio.site` links).
- **Secondary Objective**: **Brand Authority & Cultural Elevation** (Maintaining industry leadership in visual design aesthetics and creator enablement).

---

## 2. Channel Role & Resource Allocation
- **Instagram (`@squarespace`) [Primary / 60% Effort]**: Visual proof, creator storytelling, design teardowns, and primary link gateway.
- **YouTube [Secondary / 20% Effort]**: Tutorials, Fluid Engine masterclasses, long-form creator documentaries.
- **TikTok [Secondary / 15% Effort]**: Fast website build time-lapses, creator struggle relatability, Bio Site hacks.
- **LinkedIn [Tertiary / 5% Effort]**: B2B commerce announcements, leadership perspectives, agency partner features.

---

## 3. Strategic Wedge
- *"Design as a Commercial Superpower."*
- Unapologetic focus on editorial aesthetics, bespoke typography, and friction-free creator commerce tools, differentiating from utilitarian SaaS competitors.

---

## 4. Content Pillars & Allocation
1. **Creator Showcases & Case Studies (40%)**: Real customer businesses, portfolio tours, and revenue growth stories.
2. **Product Feature & AI Demos (30%)**: Fluid Engine drag-and-drop walkthroughs, AI website generation, mobile responsive setups.
3. **Actionable Design & Conversion Education (20%)**: High-save typography, palette, and landing page optimization carousels.
4. **Cultural Campaigns & Brand POV (10%)**: Cinematic manifestos and celebrity/director collaborations.

---

## 5. North Star Goal & KPIs
- **North Star Metric**: **Profile Conversion Rate (Profile Visits $\rightarrow$ Bio Site Link Clicks)** with a target of **8.5%**.
- **Supporting KPIs**:
  - Educational carousel save rate (>1,200 saves/post).
  - Reel 3-second completion rate (>42%).
  - Monthly trial sign-ups initiated via social (>12,000/month).
