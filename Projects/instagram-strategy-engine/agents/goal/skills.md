# Agent Skills: Goal (Goals & KPIs)

## 1. Assigned Skills & Capabilities
- **Goal & Target-Setting Foundation Skill (`goal-skill.md`)**:
  - *Description*: Architect of the NORTH framework, defining business cascades, channel North Stars, SMART objectives, benchmark-calibrated targets, and action thresholds.
- **NORTH Metric Framework & Funnel Mapping**:
  - *Description*: Translates full-funnel customer stages (Awareness $\rightarrow$ Consideration $\rightarrow$ Conversion $\rightarrow$ Retention) into single-channel North Stars.
- **Anti-Gaming & Quality Guardrail Pairing**:
  - *Description*: Couples volume/growth metrics with ratio-based quality floors (e.g., follower targets paired with minimum engagement/save rates).
- **Threshold & Contingency Trigger Design**:
  - *Description*: Formulates diagnostic rules that trigger tactical pivots when performance deviates from expected bounds.

## 2. Tools & MCP Integrations
- [x] Workspace File Access (Reading `brand-profile.md`, `social-strategy.md`, and writing `goals-and-kpis.md`)
- [x] Benchmark Research & Industry Norm Lookups (Referencing verified engagement and conversion baselines)
- [x] Structured Goal Interviewing (Interactive elicitation of baseline numbers and business deadlines)

## 3. Skill Execution Workflow
1. **Step 0 (Read Strategy Foundation)**: Load `brand-profile.md` and `social-strategy.md`.
2. **Step 1 (Name Business Goal)**: Anchor social initiatives to a core business outcome (awareness, leads, sales, retention).
3. **Step 2 (Channel North Stars)**: Assign exactly one North Star metric per active channel + 1–2 supporting metrics.
4. **Step 3 (Render SMART)**: Formulate targets with exact numbers, timeframes, and implementation methods.
5. **Step 4 (Benchmark & Guardrail Setting)**: Calibrate targets against actual historical baselines (or set learning goals) and attach quality guardrails.
6. **Step 5 (Hold to 3–5 & Set Triggers)**: Cap KPIs at 3–5, specify action thresholds, establish review cadence, and hand off to `analytics-and-reporting`.
