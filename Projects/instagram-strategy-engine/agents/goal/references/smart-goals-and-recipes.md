## 2. Worked Recipe: B2B Instagram Inbound Funnel

### Step 1: Business Outcome
- **Business Goal**: Generate $30,000 in new monthly pipeline for marketing automation consulting.

### Step 2: SMART Objective
- **Objective**: Generate 45 qualified inbound DM inquiries per month by end of Q2, converting into at least 15 booked discovery calls, using 4 weekly carousel teardowns with keyword comment triggers.

### Step 3: Cascade & Channel North Star
- **Channel**: Instagram
- **North Star Metric**: Qualified Comment-to-DM Inbound Leads (Target: 45/month).
- **Supporting Metric 1 (Quality Guardrail)**: Qualified Lead Ratio $\ge 70\%$ (Verified agency or business owner).
- **Supporting Metric 2 (Reach Input)**: Save Rate $\ge 4.0\%$ across all carousel posts.

### Step 4: Action Thresholds
- *Threshold A*: If comment volume is high ($>100$/post) but DM conversion is $<20\%$ $\rightarrow$ Revise automated DM greeting copy and qualification flow.
- *Threshold B*: If save rate drops below $3.0\%$ $\rightarrow$ Increase depth and technical specificity of carousel slides.
