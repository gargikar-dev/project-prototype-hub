# Content Pillars Examples Across Business Archetypes

---

## Example 1: B2B Instagram Automation Platform

### ❌ Useless Generic Buckets
- Bucket 1: Educational Posts
- Bucket 2: Motivational Quotes
- Bucket 3: Behind the Scenes
- Bucket 4: Promotional Posts

### ✅ Actionable Strategic Content Pillars
1. **Pillar 1 (Signature Theme - 40%)**: *"The Death of Manual DMs & The Rise of Conversational Inbound."*
   - *Why We Own It*: We automate $1M+ in pipeline and believe manual DM prospecting is broken.
   - *Angles*: Why 20 cold DMs/day kills your account; The 3-second comment-to-DM trigger mechanism; Why response latency loses 60% of leads.
2. **Pillar 2 (Audience Pain - 30%)**: *"Creator & Agency Workflow Bottlenecks."*
   - *Why We Own It*: Solves the #1 complaint of solo founders drowning in admin tasks.
   - *Angles*: How to handle 500 reel comments in 0 seconds; Why lead magnets stall in bio links; Fixing broken ManyChat flows.
3. **Pillar 3 (Credibility / Teardowns - 20%)**: *"Live Funnel Teardowns & Architecture Audits."*
   - *Why We Own It*: Live system breakdowns showing actual architecture nodes and logic.
   - *Angles*: Auditing a 7-figure creator's DM funnel; Step-by-step trigger blueprint for webinar registrations.
4. **Pillar 4 (Conversion & Proof - 10%)**: *"Client Pipeline Case Studies & Lead Magnet Drops."*
   - *Why We Own It*: Direct proof of revenue and automated bookings.
   - *Angles*: How Agency Z generated 34 sales calls in 48 hours; Free Automation Blueprint download guide.
