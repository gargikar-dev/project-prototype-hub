# Agent Skills: Content Pillars

## 1. Assigned Skills & Capabilities
- **Content Pillars Foundation Skill (`content-pillar-skill.md`)**:
  - *Description*: Derives, validates, and balances 3–5 ownable subject themes from brand strategy and audience research into `content-pillars.md`.
- **Subject vs. Format/Intent Deconstruction**:
  - *Description*: Translates ambiguous user requests and generic buckets into crisp, ownable subject areas.
- **The 4-Pillar Test Validation**:
  - *Description*: Evaluates candidate themes across Ownability, Audience Relevance, Strategic Value, and Idea Sustainability.
- **Content Mix & Thematic Distribution Architecture**:
  - *Description*: Establishes strategic percentage weightings across signature, authority, pain-point, and conversion themes.

## 2. Tools & MCP Integrations
- [x] Workspace File Access (Reading `brand-profile.md`, `audience.md`, `social-strategy.md` and writing `content-pillars.md`)
- [x] Brainstorming & Idea Expansion Tools (Generating 8–12 candidate themes and testing 10+ sub-topic angles per pillar)
- [x] Trend & Competitor Analysis (Validating pillar distinctiveness against industry noise)

## 3. Skill Execution Workflow
1. **Step 0 (Read Foundation)**: Ingest `brand-profile.md`, `audience.md`, and `social-strategy.md`.
2. **Step 1 (Mine Strategy Inputs)**: Extract positioning/POV, audience JTBD/pains, proof/expertise, and core offers.
3. **Step 2 (Generate Candidate Themes)**: Brainstorm 8–12 candidate subject themes.
4. **Step 3 (The Pillar Test)**: Filter candidates down to 3–5 themes passing Ownable, Relevant, Strategic, and Sustainable criteria.
5. **Step 4 (Pillar Specification)**: Detail each pillar with name, objective, target segment, proof points, and 5–8 sub-topic angles.
6. **Step 5 (Mix Balancing & Synthesis)**: Design the percentage mix, highlight the signature pillar, and compile `content-pillars.md`.
