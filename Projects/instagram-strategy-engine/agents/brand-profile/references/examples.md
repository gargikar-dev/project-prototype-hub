# Example Brand Profiles

Reference examples across different business archetypes to guide brand profile creation.

---

## Example 1: B2B Growth Automation SaaS

```markdown
# Brand Profile: WorkflowPilot

## 1. Identity & One-Liner
- **Business Name**: WorkflowPilot
- **One-Sentence Description**: WorkflowPilot automates multi-channel social publishing and CRM lead attribution for digital marketing agencies.
- **Category**: B2B Marketing Automation
- **Tagline**: "Scale agency output without scaling payroll."

## 2. Positioning & Point of View
- **The One Differentiator**: Unified automation pipeline connecting Instagram carousels directly to Stripe attribution.
- **Staked-Out Beliefs**: Agencies shouldn't spend 20 hours a week doing copy-paste publishing.
- **Rejected Norms**: Disconnected micro-tools and black-box vanity metrics.

## 3. Target Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Agency operations leads and social media directors.
- **Attention Triggers**: Workflow automation diagrams, tool stack audits, time-saving breakdowns.

## 4. Voice, Tone & Lexicon
- **Tone**: Pragmatic, expert, dry wit, highly technical yet readable.
- **Formality**: 3/5 | **Energy**: 2/5 | **Humor**: 3/5 (dry)
- **Preferred Words**: pipeline, leverage, throughput, deterministic, attribution.
- **Banned Words**: revolutionary, magical, secret sauce, game-changer.

## 5. Proof & Offers
- **Proof**: 2.4M posts automated, 450+ agency partners, avg. 14 hrs/week saved per team.
- **Offer**: "Try the agency workflow starter kit free for 14 days."

## 6. Guardrails & Operational Defaults
- **Guardrails**: No unsubstantiated ROI claims; always mention platform API compliance.
- **AI Policy**: AI-assisted asset creation allowed with human editorial signoff.
- **Languages**: English (US).
```

## Example 2: Creator / Personal Brand Consultant

```markdown
# Brand Profile: Alex Rivera (Creator Ops)

## 1. Identity & One-Liner
- **Business Name**: Alex Rivera / Creator Ops
- **One-Sentence Description**: Systems consulting helping 7-figure creators turn chaotic personal brands into automated media companies.
- **Category**: Creator Economy Consulting

## 2. Positioning & Point of View
- **The One Differentiator**: Deep operational backend systems rather than generic growth coaching.
- **Staked-Out Beliefs**: Burnout is an operations failure, not a work ethic problem.
- **Rejected Norms**: "Post 5x a day until you drop" hustle culture.

## 3. Target Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Full-time creators and boutique media operators with 100k+ audiences.

## 4. Voice & Tone
- **Tone**: Direct, conversational, vulnerable yet authoritative, punchy 1-line observations.
- **Banned Words**: guru, ninja, monetize your passion, synergy.
```
