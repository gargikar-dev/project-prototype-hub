# Audience & Attention Framework

Social audiences are not demographic spreadsheets; they are attention groups driven by specific pain points, aspirations, and triggers.

---

## 1. Social Follower vs. Commercial Buyer

- **The Practitioner / Fan (Attention Audience)**:
  - Consumes, likes, shares, bookmarks, and comments.
  - Wants educational breakdowns, actionable tactics, industry commentary, and inspiration.
- **The Decision Maker / Client (Commercial Audience)**:
  - Holds budget authority or evaluates tool purchase.
  - Wants case studies, ROI proof, operational clarity, and reliability assurances.

*Great social content speaks primarily to the practitioner while embedding proof points that reassure the decision maker.*

---

## 2. Attention Triggers

Identify what causes the target audience to stop scrolling:
- **Pain Callouts**: "Still spending 3 hours scheduling Reels manually?"
- **Unpopular Truths**: "Why 90% of automated DM flows get ignored."
- **Tear-Downs & Frameworks**: "Deconstructing how [Brand] reached $10M ARR."
- **Swipeable Swipe Files**: "5 prompt architectures we use daily."

---

## 3. Audience Segmentation Across Business Types

- **B2B SaaS / Services**: Focus on practitioner daily struggles, operational bottlenecks, career upside.
- **E-Commerce / D2C**: Focus on lifestyle aspiration, aesthetic affinity, problem-solving product features, social proof.
- **Creators / Coaches**: Focus on transformation, mindset shifts, behind-the-scenes building, relatable struggles.
- **Local Business**: Focus on local community pride, customer spotlights, behind-the-scenes craftsmanship.
