# Voice & Lexicon Framework

Capturing a brand's voice requires moving past vague adjectives like "authentic", "friendly", and "professional". This guide provides the structured dimensions and extraction tools to establish a distinct voice profile.

---

## 1. The Four Core Voice Dimensions (1 to 5 Scales)

1. **Formality Level**:
   - `1`: Hyper-casual / Internet slang / lowercase / conversational snippets.
   - `3`: Polished conversational / smart casual / professional peer.
   - `5`: Academic / Corporate / Strict institutional prose.

2. **Energy & Temperature**:
   - `1`: Calm, understated, minimalist, deadpan.
   - `3`: Measured enthusiasm, confident clarity.
   - `5`: High-energy, hype, exclamation-driven, urgent.

3. **Humor & Wit**:
   - `1`: Completely serious, direct, literal.
   - `3`: Subtle dry wit, relatable observations.
   - `5`: Satirical, meme-heavy, roast-friendly, bold.

4. **Stance & Authority**:
   - `1`: Curious explorer / student / sharing lessons learned in real-time.
   - `3`: Seasoned practitioner / practitioner peer.
   - `5`: Definitive expert / thought leader / category standard.

---

## 2. The Lexicon Matrix

Every brand voice must define:
- **Preferred Power Words**: Signature terminology, specific technical vocabulary, or community phrases that signal belonging.
- **Banned Words & Buzzwords**: Words that dilute brand authority (e.g., *synergy, revolutionize, delve, leverage, unlock your potential*).
- **Sentence Architecture & Cadence**: Short punchy lines vs. rhythmic multi-clause structures; rules for fragments, parentheses, and line breaks.

---

## 3. Signature Rhetorical Devices
- **The Contrarian Opener**: "Most founders obsess over X. The best ones ignore it completely."
- **The Before-and-After Parallelism**: "Novice mistake: X. Expert approach: Y."
- **The Single-Line Punchline**: A standalone line that delivers the core takeaway after a detailed breakdown.
- **The Concrete Receipt**: Immediately backing up an assertion with a number or case reference.
